The Heart of the Blossoms

A kingdom deeply rooted in heritage, blooming with natural wonder.

Our Heritage

The Kingdom of Cheria is more than a geographic location; it is a living tapestry woven from centuries of reverence for nature. Our ancestors understood that the delicate cherry blossom—ephemeral yet enduring in its return—is a reflection of life itself.

We have built our society upon the foundation of this natural philosophy. Our architecture seamlessly blends the strength of deep, dark woods with the light, airy openness of a spring courtyard. We invite you to explore the roots of our kingdom, where tradition and modern harmony coalesce.
